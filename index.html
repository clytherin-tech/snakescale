SnakeScale Notes

Set up SnakeScale Notes

SnakeScale has no server and no account of its own. Your notes live in your own Google Drive, in a folder named SnakeScale Notes, and attachments live in an attachments subfolder of that same folder.

Sign in with Google is the only sign in. There is no SnakeScale app password. Continue to the Google sign in step and approve access; SnakeScale then connects Drive and creates your first notebook and welcome note. Local copies on this device are encrypted with a random device key kept only on this device, which is not a login.

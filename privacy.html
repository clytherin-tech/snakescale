SnakeScale Notes

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

SnakeScale Notes is a free, open source notes app developed by Chris Gingerich ("the developer"). The app runs entirely in your web browser. The developer operates no servers for this app and does not receive, collect, or store your notes or your personal information. This policy explains what the app itself does with information, on your own device and in your own accounts.

Your notes and files

Your notebooks, notes, and attachments are stored in your own Google Drive, in a folder named "SnakeScale Notes" that the app creates for you. The app requests only the narrow Google Drive permission known as drive.file, which means it can access only the files and folders it creates. It cannot see or open anything else in your Drive. The developer has no access to your Drive, your notes, or your attachments. While the app is open, it also checks Google Drive about every 30 seconds for changes to that folder (a feature you can turn off in Settings) and syncs only when something actually changed.

Microphone and voice notes

The app uses your microphone only when you choose to record a voice note, and only after your browser asks for your permission. The finished recording is stored in your own Google Drive as an attachment, exactly like any other file you attach to a note. Turning speech into text is done by your browser's built in speech recognition service, provided by your browser's maker (for example Microsoft in Edge or Google in Chrome). The app itself sends nothing anywhere for transcription, and the typed words it produces are saved only to your note, in your Drive.

A saved recording can also be transcribed later, from its card's menu. That feature loads a speech model from a public content delivery network the first time you use it. From then on the transcription runs entirely on your device: the app decodes the recording in your browser and the model reads it there. The recording itself is not sent anywhere by the app.

Information stored on your device

So the app opens quickly and works offline, it stores a small amount of information in your browser's local storage on your device: your app settings, a random encryption key created on your device, an encrypted copy of your notes and any changes waiting to sync, and your Google sign-in token kept only in encrypted form. Signing out of the app removes the cached notes, the pending changes, the sign-in token, and the encryption key from that browser. Your Google password is never seen or stored by the app; sign-in is handled entirely by Google.

Services the app contacts

The app connects only to the services listed here, and only as described:

  • Google (accounts.google.com and www.googleapis.com): for sign-in and for reading and writing your notes in your Google Drive. Google's handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Microlink (api.microlink.io): when you switch a web link inside one of your notes to its Mention or Preview display, the app sends that link's web address, and nothing else, to Microlink to fetch the page's title, description, and images. The contents of your notes are never sent. The information fetched is saved inside your note, in your Drive.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): when you use the Transcribe feature on an image, the app downloads the text recognition software from the jsDelivr content delivery network. The transcription itself runs entirely on your device; the image being transcribed is not uploaded anywhere. The same network delivers the speech recognition software when you transcribe a saved audio recording, and the model it uses is downloaded on first use; that transcription also runs entirely on your device, and the recording is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Your browser's speech recognition service: when you record a voice note in a browser that offers transcription, the audio is processed by the browser's own built in speech recognition service to turn it into text. SnakeScale Notes does not operate that service and does not receive the audio; the recording itself is saved only to your Google Drive, as an attachment.
  • GitHub Pages: the app's program files are hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may record standard request information when your browser loads the app, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

What the app does not do

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. No personal information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing. No human at the developer reads your notes.

Task reminder notifications are generated entirely on your own device by your browser; nothing about your tasks is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Google API disclosure

SnakeScale Notes' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information accessed through Google Drive is used only to create, read, update, and delete your own notes and attachments, at your direction, in your own Drive account.

Your choices and control

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from the third-party access page of your Google Account settings. Deleting the "SnakeScale Notes" folder in your Google Drive deletes the notes and attachments the app stored there. Clearing the app's site data in your browser removes everything the app stored on that device.

Children

SnakeScale Notes is a general purpose note taking tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13.
